Privacy Policy

Ninja Icon Tray

Last updated: 2026

Summary

Ninja Icon Tray does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app works completely offline and sends no information to any server.

Data that is processed

  • App preferences (language, keyboard shortcuts, title bar height, button width, active/paused state): stored only locally on your device, in the application data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Ninja Icon Tray\). They never leave your device.
  • System windows: to minimize and restore windows, the app interacts with the open windows on your device (position, title and icon). This information is used only in the moment, in memory, to perform the requested action, and is not stored or transmitted.

Data that is NOT collected

  • No personally identifiable data is collected.
  • No advertising or trackers.
  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No network connections are made for the app to function.

Permissions

The app runs with full trust (runFullTrust) because it needs to interact with other applications' windows and with the system tray. This permission is used exclusively for the minimize/restore features described in the Store listing.

Legal documents opened in the browser

If you open the EULA or this Policy from the app, it will try to show the online version (if you have a connection) or otherwise a local copy in your browser. No data of yours is sent when doing so.
